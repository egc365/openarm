Public motor face used here:
Pololu file 0J949, Micro Metal Gearmotors with carbon brushes (HPCB), no encoder, drawing date 03 April 2024.
https://www.pololu.com/file/0j949/micro-metal-gearmotor-dimensions.pdf
Face 12.0 mm by 10.0 mm. Shaft diameter 3.0 mm. Two M1.6 holes spaced 9.0 mm.
Gearbox length L is 9 mm except 12.5 mm at 1000:1.
Motor can label 15.4 mm. Overall length maximum 25.6 mm except 29.1 mm at 1000:1.
A 10 mm dimension and a 5 mm dimension are on the sheet and were not assembled into a side view.

Public tiny servo that is not the owner's repository:
CaptainObvious, Thingiverse thing 4030416, 2019-12-08, CC BY.
Parts named on that page: N20 geared motor, AS5600, 40 tooth GT2 belt, pulley for a 5 mm or 6 mm magnet, one 3x7x3 mm bearing, M2 screws, and an external driver.
Housing envelope and center distance are not in that page text, so they were not drawn.

Clamp drawn here, our choices labeled as choices:
Plate 16.0 mm by 14.0 mm by 3.0 mm.
Two 1.8 mm clearance holes, 9.0 mm apart.
Center bore 3.4 mm for the 3.0 mm shaft.
The plate does not enclose gears. It has no load rating.

A 5 lb figure is not computed. No melt test was run.
