# Questions to ask a person who has broken these parts

Taken from the forum reports in `references.md`. Ask them in this order. Write the answer next to the question. Do not fill an answer from a guess.

1. Did the plastic gears fuse after a long stall, or after the supply voltage went above the servo rating? Revolio_ClockbergJr reported both on an SG90.
2. Can the output turn by hand through a full circle, or is there a dead spot where a tooth is gone? totalJTM warned that turning a cheap servo by hand can strip the teeth. Stop if the horn does not move freely.
3. What filament was printed, and how large is one tooth? NZRVA told a melted PLA gear train to leave PLA, use a coarser tooth, and add grease.
4. Did the case smell of hot plastic while the gear was noisy? IvorTheEngine traced that pair of signs to friction, not to a lack of wall thickness.
5. What is the measured center distance of the belt or gear pair, and what backlash remains after the grease is on? The Thingiverse 4030416 page text does not give this number.
6. What mass was hung, at what distance from the shaft, and what was the case temperature when the plastic first yielded? A 5 lb claim is not a substitute for that row.
7. For the shoulder pad, does a pull along the horn axis lift the horn off the pad? If it lifts, the part is not a clip, whatever the file name says.

8. On the arm in front of you, which joint number flexes the wrist, and what are the two mechanical stops in degrees? The Anvil page says OpenArm 2.0 puts flexion on J7 at -90 to +90, and puts Anvil's extra J6 travel at -45 to +70. Measure the stops. Do not copy that table onto a different arm. This question comes from the vendor page, not from a forum.
